## 6. Data collection

This section describes how paired mornings are captured, so that Lazoo cannot pre-fill the CSD-M, and how total sleep time is derived.

### Order of capture

Each morning the participant completes the CSD-M first and then Lazoo, in the same sitting. Training explains that going to bed (in_bed) and trying to sleep (sleep_attempt) are two different clock times. Completing Lazoo first is a deviation, and by default that morning cannot be evaluated for the primary endpoint.

### Lazoo

Participants make their entries on their own device, using the study code. The fields follow the data dictionary and the protocol's construct map. Edits are allowed and are kept in an audit trail to which entries can only be added, recording who made each change, what changed and when, with the original value retained, in line with the ALCOA+ principles of data integrity (attributable, legible, contemporaneous, original and accurate, plus complete, consistent, enduring and available). The data are exported in JSON and CSV formats at close-out and whenever the Chief Investigator asks. Charts are never used in place of raw timestamps. The fields vitals.core_temp[], vitals.wearable[] and ai_event[] are reserved and empty in version 1.

CSD-M field 15 (comments, `notes`) is not collected and is excluded from the case report form and the Lazoo export. The CSD-M licence terms are still to be confirmed. If the licence requires field 15, it would be kept with an instruction on the form ("Do not enter names, contact details or other identifying information; describe sleep only"), a limit of 200 characters, a redaction review at upload and the removal of identifiers from free text, and it would be covered in the DPIA.

### CSD-M

The source is a locked PDF or electronic form, uploaded electronically by the participant and carrying the study ID only, with no names, dates of birth or identifying free text. The licensed instrument will be used once the Carney industry licence has been filed. No paper copies are posted and no photographs are taken. Values are transcribed onto the daily construct page of the case report form by double entry, and discrepancies are logged and resolved before database lock.

### Derived quantities

Derived quantities are calculated on export or in the analysis file, and the raw clock times are stored as well.

- TWAK = out_of_bed − wake
- TST = (out_of_bed − sleep_attempt) − SOL − WASO − TWAK  (PRIMARY; not CSD-M item 8)
- TIB = out_of_bed − in_bed
- SE = TST / TIB × 100
- sleep_onset = sleep_attempt + SOL

Total sleep time (TST) is the primary quantity; it is calculated as above and is not taken from CSD-M item 8. These formulas for total sleep time, sleep onset latency, wake after sleep onset and sleep efficiency are draft study arithmetic for this agreement study and are not a product claim. The sponsor will set the product measure separately.

A paired morning can be evaluated for the primary endpoint when both diaries record sleep_attempt, sleep onset latency, wake after sleep onset, wake and out_of_bed, these are internally consistent, and total sleep time is at least zero. Naps are excluded from night-time total sleep time, and wake after sleep onset excludes the final waking.

### Usability

The System Usability Scale (Brooke, 1996) and three structured questions are recorded on the case report form on day 15. They are entered once, and no score is shown to the participant in the app as a clinical output.

### Consent

The version of the wording for each consent choice is stored. Withdrawal of consent choice 3 stops future research use, and the participant information sheet explains that a locked analysis set is not reopened. Consent choice 4 is off by default.

### Electronic case report form

The default collection model is a paper case report form together with the Lazoo export. The supplier of any electronic case report form, and its validation, are still to be confirmed: [PLACEHOLDER: electronic case report form supplier, if one is used]. Validation would follow the MHRA "GXP" Data Integrity Guidance and Definitions (March 2018) and ICH E6(R3) on computerised systems, with 21 CFR Part 11 applied only where a submission in the United States is planned.

## 8. Quality control

The checks below support measurement agreement and are not aimed at diagnostic accuracy.

- **Consent.** Every completer (100%) has consent choices 1 and 3, the version of the wording and the date recorded.
- **Pairing log.** Every morning is coded as able to be evaluated or not, with a deviation code where needed.
- **CSD-M transcription.** Double entry is completed and the discrepancy log closed before database lock.
- **Lazoo.** The export contains raw timestamps; in_bed is distinct from sleep_attempt; edits carry an audit trail; and the reserved fields of version 1 are empty.
- **Derived total sleep time.** Total sleep time is recomputed independently from the raw clock times in the analysis file. Any total sleep time pre-computed by the app, if one were ever present, is not relied on; version 1 is not expected to display a clinical score.
- **Range checks.** Sleep onset latency, wake after sleep onset and time awake after final waking are each at least zero; out_of_bed ≥ wake ≥ sleep_attempt; time in bed is greater than zero for sleep efficiency; and total sleep time below zero or above time in bed is flagged for a query. These queries are data queries and are never shown to the participant as clinical flags.
- **System Usability Scale.** All 10 items are present, or the missing items are explained; the score is calculated centrally.
- **App version.** The app version on each export matches the version recorded in the software version log. That version is still to be confirmed.

**Database lock.** The Chief Investigator and the data manager, once named, sign to confirm that pairing, transcription and export receipts are complete. The database is unlocked only with a reason recorded in a file note.

Quality control is aimed at accurate transcription and never at "correcting" diaries towards a diagnosis. Queries are resolved against the source, meaning the participant's worksheet or the Lazoo audit trail, and never through a clinician's interpretation.
