## Invitation

You are invited to take part in a research study about a digital sleep and lifestyle diary app (Lazoo). This sheet should take about 15 minutes. Please ask questions before you decide.

## What is the app?

Lazoo is intended to **record and display** your own sleep and lifestyle information for your review and, if you choose, to share with a clinician. **It does not diagnose, predict, treat, or determine the management of any medical condition.**

## Why is the study being done?

Sleep clinics often use paper sleep diaries. This study asks whether a digital diary is:

- easy to use
- feasible to complete each day for 14 days
- in agreement with a standard paper Consensus Sleep Diary, and for some people with a wrist-worn actigraphy device

The study does **not** test whether the app can diagnose a sleep disorder, speed up diagnosis, or improve NHS diagnostic pathways. Those claims have been removed from this sheet.

## Why have I been invited?

You are attending the UCLH Sleep Unit and have reported sleep difficulties. We plan to invite about 50 adults aged 18–75. This study is for adults only. It is not a school or children’s study.

## Do I have to take part?

No. Care at UCLH does not change if you say no or if you withdraw later. If you withdraw, we will keep data already collected up to that point, as explained below, unless you ask us to stop using identifiable data where the law allows.

## What will happen if I take part?

Your involvement lasts **14 days**.

**At the start**

- A researcher will go through this sheet with you and take written consent
- You will complete a short questionnaire about your sleep
- We will show you the digital diary, the paper Consensus Sleep Diary, and (if you are in that part of the study) an actigraphy device worn like a watch

**During the 14 days**

- Complete the digital diary each morning
- Complete the paper diary each morning
- If selected, wear the actigraphy device continuously for 14 days

**At the end**

- Return the paper diary and the actigraphy device (if used)
- Complete a System Usability Scale questionnaire
- You may be invited to a short optional interview about your experience

## Possible benefits

You may not benefit personally. The study may help researchers understand whether a digital diary is usable as a **record**, compared with paper. It will not diagnose you and it will not speed up your NHS diagnosis.

## Possible risks

Risks are expected to be low. The app takes a little time each day. The actigraphy watch is worn on the wrist; if your skin becomes irritated, take it off and tell the research team. Technical problems with the app can happen; the team will help if they can.

## Confidentiality and data protection

UCLH is the **sponsor** and the **data controller** for this study. Sleep and Health Clinic App Ltd and Sleep & Health Clinic Ltd are **not** the controller for this UCLH study.

UCLH will use your information to run the study. Your name and other identifiers will not be shared outside the research team except as required for Trust research governance, audit, or the law.

**Retention:** the 0.1 draft said UCLH would keep identifiable information for 2 years after the study ends. That period must be confirmed with UCLH R&D before this sheet is issued (it may need to be longer to match Trust policy). Pseudonymised research data will be kept for the period in the approved protocol.

Lawful basis (draft, for R&D/DPO): public task / research in the public interest, with explicit consent for health data (UK GDPR Articles 6 and 9). Confirm with the UCLH DPO before issue.
