# SOP-CMF-001 — Management of the Lazoo Clinical Master File

**Version:** 0.1 draft  
**Effective:** pending CI sign-off  
**Applies to:** all Lazoo validation studies and ongoing clinical research

## 1. Purpose

Keep a reconstructable record of how Lazoo research was designed, approved, run, and reported. Lazoo is SaMD Class IIa (class 2a). This SOP does not mark the file UKCA, CE, or compliant.

## 2. Scope

In: protocols, approvals, consent, data dictionaries, validation reports, delegation, correspondence that changes a study, and the intended-purpose note. The purpose is not locked. Purpose: TBD — pending Sara paste.

Out: marketing drafts, competitor research, and clinic operational SOPs unless they are study-specific. Device-file drafts are not waiting for a second layer. This SOP does not write them and does not mark them done.

## 4. Naming

`LAZ-<TYPE>-<NNN>[-<doc>]_v<n.n>.<ext>`

Examples:

- `LAZ-VAL-001-protocol_v0.1.md`
- `LAZ-IP-001-intended-purpose_v1.0.md`
- `LAZ-RWE-001-PIS_v0.1.pdf`

Study IDs are issued on the study register and never reused.

## 5. Versioning

- Drafts start at 0.1. First CI-approved issue is 1.0.
- Superseded files stay in the folder, marked superseded, never deleted.
- The index records the current version of each controlled document.
- Changes to a filed document must be traceable (who, when, what).

## 6. Filing rule

A document is “filed” when it is in the correct folder, named, dated, and listed on the index or the study checklist. Emails that change design, consent, or approvals are saved into `06-correspondence/` and cross-referenced.

## 7. Retention

LAZ-VAL-001 and LAZ-RWE-001 are **not** CTIMPs, so the 25-year statutory TMF clock in the amended Clinical Trials Regulations does not apply by default.

SHC policy for Lazoo research (good practice, pending CI sign-off):

- Keep essential records for **at least 10 years** after the study report is signed, or longer if a licence contract, REC, or later SaMD submission needs it.
- Keep participant-level research data for the period stated in the PIS / DPIA.
- There is one layer. Do not freeze a copy into a second product folder. Pack stays OPEN. Do not mark a study UKCA or CE.
