## 2. Background and Rationale

People who keep a sleep diary, and clinicians who later read one, need a digital log that captures the same constructs as the field’s consensus paper diary and that stores those entries with timestamps, an audit trail and an export of raw values. The Consensus Sleep Diary–Morning (CSD-M) is the reference self-report instrument for prospective sleep monitoring (Carney et al., 2012). Digital implementations are not automatically equivalent: field names, order, derived indices and edit behaviour can all change what is recorded.

Lazoo is software as a medical device at the Medicines and Healthcare products Regulatory Agency (MHRA) Class IIa (class 2a) working position, with one layer. Its intended purpose is insomnia monitoring only, as decided by the sponsor on 13 October 2026, with no therapy claims. Accurate, complete and auditable diary records are a precondition for any monitoring use of the diary. This study tests whether Lazoo records the same night-level sleep quantities as a concurrently completed CSD-M, and whether adults can use the app for that task.

Calculated total sleep time is the primary quantity because CSD-M item 8 is a subjective estimate of time asleep and is not the derived index used for diary total sleep time in this study (see Endpoints). Reed and Sacco (2016) and Maich et al. (2018) show that total sleep time and sleep efficiency change if time in bed is taken from lights-out rather than from the later “tried to sleep” clock. LAZ-VAL-001 therefore keeps in_bed (CSD-M item 1 construct) and sleep_attempt (item 2 construct) as separate fields and uses the formula in Endpoints unless the Chief Investigator signs a documented Maich variant in the statistical analysis plan.

This is not a clinical trial of an investigational medicinal product. Whether the study is a clinical investigation under regulation 16 of the UK Medical Devices Regulations 2002 is to be confirmed (see Ethics and Regulatory Considerations). This protocol makes no statement about conformity marking. An artificial intelligence validation report and a clinical evaluation report are outside this protocol.

Studies sponsored by other organisations are outside this protocol. Their documents, letterhead, participant information and contact routes must not be merged into this file.

## 4. Study Objectives

### Primary objective

To estimate agreement between Lazoo and concurrently completed CSD-M for calculated total sleep time (total sleep time) over paired mornings in adults using the restricted research app.

### Secondary objectives

1. To estimate agreement for sleep onset latency (SOL), wake after sleep onset (WASO), sleep efficiency (SE) and number of awakenings (excluding the final awakening).
2. To describe completeness (paired mornings; item-level missingness) over 14 mornings.
3. To describe timestamp integrity and audit-trail completeness on Lazoo edits (who, what, when; original value retained).
4. To describe usability of Lazoo v1 using the System Usability Scale (Brooke, 1996) and three structured questions.

### Exploratory objectives

The study will also describe the difference between calculated total sleep time and the CSD-M item-8 construct (subjective estimated time asleep; tst_subjective); item 8 is not the primary endpoint. It will describe nap and doze reporting and lifestyle text (caffeine, alcohol and sleep medication as user-entered text) as covariates only.

### What this study will not do

This study will not establish or support a diagnostic claim or a therapy claim. It will not test cognitive behavioural therapy for insomnia (CBT-I), treatment response or care pathways, and it will not assess obstructive sleep apnoea (OSA). It will not create a national registry or validate an artificial intelligence (AI) output, and it will not recruit participants from any NHS organisation's patient lists, or from Sleep & Health Clinic Ltd (08904119) patients.

## 5. Study Design

Prospective, paired, within-person comparison. Each morning for 14 consecutive mornings the participant completes CSD-M first (locked PDF or electronic form only; no paper copies are posted) and then Lazoo, in the same sitting, so Lazoo cannot auto-fill the comparator. The participant uploads each completed CSD-M PDF electronically. Every participant follows the same procedure without randomisation; no investigational medicinal product is used, care is unchanged, and the app records entries without interpreting them.

## 12. Endpoints

For this agreement study, calculated total sleep time is the primary study quantity. It is not a product claim. The two bedtime clock times are kept separate, and item 8 remains exploratory.

### Derived quantities (both diaries, same formula)

Clock times are datetimes on the diary_date (with the previous calendar day allowed for in_bed and sleep_attempt). Durations are in minutes.

- TWAK = out_of_bed − wake
- TST = (out_of_bed − sleep_attempt) − SOL − WASO − TWAK
- TIB = out_of_bed − in_bed
- SE = TST / TIB × 100
- sleep_onset (derived, not asked as a clock time) = sleep_attempt + SOL

in_bed and sleep_attempt must not be collapsed. Naps and dozes do not enter nocturnal total sleep time. Wake after sleep onset is the total of awakenings excluding the final wake. Primary TWAK uses out_of_bed − wake, not CSD-M item 6b. Item 6b (twak_try_min) may be used only in a pre-specified sensitivity analysis if collected.

A primary-evaluable paired morning has, on both diaries, sleep_attempt, sleep onset latency, wake after sleep onset, wake and out_of_bed, all internally consistent (out_of_bed ≥ wake ≥ sleep_attempt; sleep onset latency ≥ 0; wake after sleep onset ≥ 0; total sleep time ≥ 0). in_bed is required for time in bed and SE secondary analyses and is still collected every morning.

### Primary endpoint

Night-level difference in calculated total sleep time: Lazoo minus CSD-M, in minutes. Analysis: Bland–Altman mean bias and 95% limits of agreement, with nights clustered within participant. No diagnostic cut-point. No claim that Lazoo is valid for diagnosing insomnia or any other condition.

### Secondary endpoints

- Bland–Altman (and ICC, method in SAP) for SOL, WASO, SE and awakenings_n.
- Completeness: proportion of mornings with both diaries; item-level missingness; proportion of participants who are completers (at least 10 of 14 evaluable paired mornings).
- Timestamp and audit: proportion of Lazoo entries with client and server timestamps; proportion of edits with who, what and when recorded and the original value retained.
- SUS total score (0–100); descriptive summaries of the three structured questions.

### Exploratory

Calculated total sleep time versus tst_subjective (CSD-M item 8 construct) on each diary; between-diary difference on item 8. Lifestyle text and naps as covariates or descriptors only.

### Sample size

Feasibility floor: 30 completers. This is not the final n. The signed statistical analysis plan will calculate n, with a sleep statistician, so that the 95% confidence interval around the total sleep time limits of agreement is usable. Until the statistical analysis plan is signed, 30 is described as a feasibility floor rather than a powered Bland–Altman sample.

To be confirmed: signed statistical analysis plan and statistician appointment.

## 15. Statistical Analysis Plan

A separate signed statistical analysis plan will be filed before database lock and before any ethics submission. Until then, the following is sufficient to design the case report form and data management plan but is not a substitute for the statistical analysis plan.

Three analysis populations are defined: (i) all consented participants; (ii) completers (at least 10 of 14 evaluable paired mornings), who form the feasibility population; and (iii) all evaluable paired mornings. The primary analysis is a Bland–Altman analysis of night-level calculated total sleep time (Lazoo − CSD-M), reporting the mean bias, the standard deviation (SD) of the differences and the 95% limits of agreement. Clustering of nights within person will be handled by a method named in the statistical analysis plan (for example Bland and Altman 2007 repeated-measures limits, or a mixed-effects residual SD).

Secondary agreement uses the same approach for sleep onset latency, wake after sleep onset, sleep efficiency and awakenings_n, with the intraclass correlation coefficient (ICC) for absolute agreement specified in the statistical analysis plan. Completeness, timestamps and System Usability Scale results are reported descriptively, without multiplicity adjustment unless the statistical analysis plan adds one. The primary analysis uses evaluable paired mornings, and clock times are not imputed for it unless the statistical analysis plan defines a rule. The analysis includes no diagnostic cut-points, receiver operating characteristic (ROC) curves or diagnostic-accuracy analyses. The software will be named in the statistical analysis plan.

The analysis is conducted by or for the sponsor. Results and analysis files belong to the sponsor (see Intellectual Property and Data Ownership).
