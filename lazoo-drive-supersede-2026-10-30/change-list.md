## 1. What leaves the phone

**Old**

Heartbeats are detected on the patch; heart rate and the core temperature estimate are worked out on the patient's phone, and the data leave the phone only when the patient chooses to share their data for a report, and the patient and their clinician then see the results in that shared report.

**New**

Heartbeats are detected on the patch; heart rate and the core temperature estimate are worked out on the patient's phone. The core temperature estimate stays on the phone and is to be shown there, and it stays out of the shared report, which holds recorded data only. The recorded heart rate leaves the phone only when the patient chooses to share their data for a report, and the patient and their clinician then see that recorded heart rate in the shared report.

**Why this sentence**

The old sentence sends the core temperature estimate out of the phone in the shared report. Sara McNeillis's answers of 26 October 2026 rule that out. The estimate is worked out on the phone, it is to be shown on the phone, and the shared report holds recorded data only. The recorded heart rate can still be shared, and the patient and the clinician see that recorded heart rate in the report.

**Sources**

- Status gate, 29 October 2026, line 16. The approved pointer says the core-temperature estimate leaves the phone in a shared report, which her answers rule out.
- Compliance-passed status, line 3. The estimate is worked out on the phone and stays out of the shared report, which holds recorded data only. Her answers settle where the estimate appears, not when.
- Intended-purpose amendment, section 12, line 238. The shared report holds only the recorded data, so the estimate no longer leaves with it. The recorded heart rate can still be shared.
- Same section, line 240. The calculation stays on the phone and out of the shared report. Her answer at 23:30 means the estimate is to be shown on the phone.
- Same section, line 252. What the person will see is the estimate on the phone, with the shared report holding the recorded data only.
- Classification review, section 9.10, line 237. The shared report holds only the recorded data, and the calculation stays on the phone and out of that report.
