## 2. Background and Rationale

People who keep a sleep diary, and clinicians who later read one, need a digital log that captures the same constructs as the field’s consensus paper diary and that stores those entries with timestamps, an audit trail and an export of raw values. The Consensus Sleep Diary–Morning (CSD-M) is the reference self-report instrument for prospective sleep monitoring (Carney et al., 2012). Digital implementations are not automatically equivalent: field names, order, derived indices and edit behaviour can all change what is recorded.

Lazoo is software as a medical device at the MHRA Class IIa (class 2a) working position, with one layer. Its draft intended purpose is insomnia diagnosis and monitoring, with no therapy claims. Accurate, complete and auditable diary records are a precondition for any monitoring use of the diary. This study tests whether Lazoo records the same night-level sleep quantities as a concurrently completed CSD-M, and whether adults can use the app for that task.

Calculated TST is the primary quantity because CSD-M item 8 is a subjective estimate of time asleep and is not the derived index used for diary TST in this study (see Endpoints). Reed and Sacco (2016) and Maich et al. (2018) show that TST and sleep efficiency change if time in bed is taken from lights-out rather than from the later “tried to sleep” clock. LAZ-VAL-001 therefore keeps in_bed (CSD-M item 1 construct) and sleep_attempt (item 2 construct) as separate fields and uses the formula in Endpoints unless the Chief Investigator signs a documented Maich variant in the SAP.

This is not a CTIMP. Whether the study is a clinical investigation under regulation 16 of the UK Medical Devices Regulations 2002 is to be confirmed (see Ethics and Regulatory Considerations). This protocol makes no statement about conformity marking. An AI validation report and a clinical evaluation report are outside this protocol.

Studies sponsored by other organisations are outside this protocol. Their documents, letterhead, participant information and contact routes must not be merged into this file.

## 4. Study Objectives

### Primary objective

To estimate agreement between Lazoo and concurrently completed CSD-M for calculated total sleep time (TST) over paired mornings in adults using the restricted research app.

### Secondary objectives

1. To estimate agreement for sleep onset latency (SOL), wake after sleep onset (WASO), sleep efficiency (SE) and number of awakenings (excluding the final awakening).
2. To describe completeness (paired mornings; item-level missingness) over 14 mornings.
3. To describe timestamp integrity and audit-trail completeness on Lazoo edits (who, what, when; original value retained).
4. To describe usability of Lazoo v1 using the System Usability Scale (Brooke, 1996) and three structured questions.

### Exploratory objectives

- To describe the difference between calculated TST and the CSD-M item-8 construct (subjective estimated time asleep; tst_subjective). Item 8 is not the primary endpoint.
- To describe nap and doze reporting and lifestyle text (caffeine, alcohol, sleep medication as user-entered text) as covariates only.

### What this study will not do

- Establish or support a diagnostic claim or a therapy claim.
- Test CBT-I, treatment response or care pathways.
- Assess OSA.
- Create a national registry.
- Validate an AI output.
- Recruit from NHS patient lists, hospital patient lists or the patient lists of Sleep and Health Clinic Ltd.

## 9. Study Procedures

1. Invitation through Prolific (default route; see Study Population) and screening against inclusion and exclusion criteria. Participant ID format: LAZ-VAL-001-NNN (never reused).
2. Informed consent. Store the consent-pack version on the user record. The research contact on all participant-facing sheets is the sponsor contact, admin@sleepandhealth.org, unless the ethics-approved documents add a Partner site contact. No personal email address and no NHS PALS line appear on sponsor documents.
3. Issue study code and login. The app must not be publicly listed. Confirm the app version against the LAZ-VER-001 pin.
4. Training (day 0): one walkthrough of the CSD-M construct fields and of the Lazoo morning diary. CSD-M is completed on paper or a locked PDF so Lazoo cannot auto-fill it. in_bed and sleep_attempt are trained as two different clock times.
5. Mornings 1–14: CSD-M first, then Lazoo, in the same sitting. No in-app interpretation, score, flag, CBT-I lesson or phenotyping output. Participants may edit Lazoo entries; edits are append-only with the original retained.
6. Day 15 (or the first working day after morning 14): return CSD-M source worksheets; complete the SUS; complete three structured usability questions; optional short interview (audio only if separately consented; default is notes on the CRF). Confirm withdrawal wishes for toggle 3.
7. Close-out: export Lazoo JSON and CSV; file the export receipt on the CRF; stop study access or leave the person on the restricted app for personal use under toggle 1 only, as they choose.

Toggle 2 (share with a nominated clinician) is optional and is not required for this study. Any share is the diary history only, with no automated interpretation.

Deviations (wrong order, Lazoo completed first, missed morning, lost PDF) are logged. A morning with Lazoo completed first is not primary-evaluable unless the SAP defines a sensitivity rule.

## 12. Endpoints

For this agreement study, calculated TST is the primary study quantity. It is not a product claim. The two bedtime clock times are kept separate, and item 8 remains exploratory.

### Derived quantities (both diaries, same formula)

Clock times are datetimes on the diary_date (with the previous calendar day allowed for in_bed and sleep_attempt). Durations are in minutes.

- TWAK = out_of_bed − wake
- TST = (out_of_bed − sleep_attempt) − SOL − WASO − TWAK
- TIB = out_of_bed − in_bed
- SE = TST / TIB × 100
- sleep_onset (derived, not asked as a clock time) = sleep_attempt + SOL

in_bed and sleep_attempt must not be collapsed. Naps and dozes do not enter nocturnal TST. WASO is the total of awakenings excluding the final wake. Primary TWAK uses out_of_bed − wake, not CSD-M item 6b. Item 6b (twak_try_min) may be used only in a pre-specified sensitivity analysis if collected.

A primary-evaluable paired morning has, on both diaries, sleep_attempt, SOL, WASO, wake and out_of_bed, all internally consistent (out_of_bed ≥ wake ≥ sleep_attempt; SOL ≥ 0; WASO ≥ 0; TST ≥ 0). in_bed is required for TIB and SE secondary analyses and is still collected every morning.

### Primary endpoint

Night-level difference in calculated TST: Lazoo minus CSD-M, in minutes. Analysis: Bland–Altman mean bias and 95% limits of agreement, with nights clustered within participant. No diagnostic cut-point. No claim that Lazoo is valid for diagnosing insomnia or any other condition.

### Secondary endpoints

- Bland–Altman (and ICC, method in SAP) for SOL, WASO, SE and awakenings_n.
- Completeness: proportion of mornings with both diaries; item-level missingness; proportion of participants who are completers (at least 10 of 14 evaluable paired mornings).
- Timestamp and audit: proportion of Lazoo entries with client and server timestamps; proportion of edits with who, what and when recorded and the original value retained.
- SUS total score (0–100); descriptive summaries of the three structured questions.

### Exploratory

Calculated TST versus tst_subjective (CSD-M item 8 construct) on each diary; between-diary difference on item 8. Lifestyle text and naps as covariates or descriptors only.

### Sample size

Feasibility floor: 30 completers. This is not the final n. The signed SAP will calculate n, with a sleep statistician, so that the 95% confidence interval around the TST limits of agreement is usable. Until the SAP is signed, 30 is not described as a powered Bland–Altman sample.

To be confirmed: signed SAP and statistician appointment.

## 15. Statistical Analysis Plan

A separate signed SAP will be filed before database lock and before any ethics submission. Until then, the following is sufficient to design the CRF and DMP but is not a substitute for the SAP.

- Analysis populations: (i) all consented; (ii) completers (at least 10 of 14 evaluable paired mornings), the feasibility population; (iii) all evaluable paired mornings.
- Primary: Bland–Altman of night-level calculated TST (Lazoo − CSD-M). Report mean bias, SD of differences and 95% limits of agreement. Clustering of nights within person will be handled by a method named in the SAP (for example Bland and Altman 2007 repeated-measures limits, or a mixed-effects residual SD).
- Secondary agreement: the same approach for SOL, WASO, SE and awakenings_n; ICC for absolute agreement, specification in the SAP.
- Completeness, timestamps and SUS: descriptive. No multiplicity adjustment unless the SAP adds one.
- Missing data: primary analysis on evaluable paired mornings. No imputation of clock times for the primary analysis unless the SAP defines a rule.
- No diagnostic cut-points, ROC curves or diagnostic-accuracy analyses.
- Software: to be named in the SAP.

The analysis is conducted by or for the sponsor. Results and analysis files belong to the sponsor (see Intellectual Property and Data Ownership).
