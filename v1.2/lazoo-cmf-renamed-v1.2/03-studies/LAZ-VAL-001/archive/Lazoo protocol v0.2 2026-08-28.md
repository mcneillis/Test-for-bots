## Background and Rationale

*Purpose:* Explain why this measurement-agreement study against the Consensus Sleep Diary is in the file. Lazoo is SaMD Class IIa (class 2a). Clinical claims: TBD — pending Sara paste.

People who keep a sleep diary, and clinicians who later read one, need a digital log that captures the same constructs as the field’s consensus paper diary and that stores those entries with timestamps, an audit trail, and an export of raw values. The Consensus Sleep Diary–Morning (CSD-M) is the reference self-report instrument for prospective sleep monitoring (Carney et al., 2012). Digital implementations are not automatically equivalent: field names, order, derived indices, and edit behaviour can all change what is recorded.

Lazoo is SaMD Class IIa (class 2a). There is one layer. Intended purpose: TBD — pending Sara paste. This study tests whether Lazoo records the same night-level sleep quantities as a concurrently completed CSD-M, and whether adults can use the app for that task. Clinical claims: TBD — pending Sara paste.

Calculated TST is the primary quantity because item 8 of CSD-M is a subjective estimate of time asleep and is not the derived index used for diary TST in the scoring lock for this study (see Endpoints). Reed and Sacco (2016) and Maich et al. (2018) document that TST and sleep efficiency move if time in bed is taken from lights-out versus from the later “tried to sleep” clock. LAZ-VAL-001 therefore keeps in_bed (CSD-M item 1 construct) and sleep_attempt (item 2 construct) as separate fields and uses the formula in Endpoints unless the Chief Investigator signs a documented Maich variant in the SAP. These TST, SOL, WASO, and SE formulas are draft study arithmetic for this agreement study. They are not the product claim. The product measure stays TBD — pending Sara paste.

Lazoo is SaMD Class IIa (class 2a). This draft does not decide whether the protocol is a UK MDR clinical investigation, and it does not mark the study UKCA, CE, or compliant. It is not a CTIMP. There is one layer, not a later different product. An AI Validation Report and a Clinical Evaluation Report are not written in this draft. Clinical claims: TBD — pending Sara paste. See LAZ-CL-001.

A UCLH-sponsored feasibility with a different sponsor, a different n, and a PIS that is not to be used here, is out of scope. Do not copy UCLH letterhead, PALS wording, or that PIS into this file.


## Study Objectives

*Purpose:* State what this agreement study will estimate. Clinical claims: TBD — pending Sara paste.

### Primary objective

To estimate agreement between Lazoo and concurrently completed CSD-M for calculated total sleep time (TST) over paired mornings in adults using the restricted research app.

### Secondary objectives

1. To estimate agreement for sleep onset latency (SOL), wake after sleep onset (WASO), sleep efficiency (SE), and number of awakenings (excluding the final awakening).
2. To describe completeness (paired mornings; item-level missingness) over 14 mornings.
3. To describe timestamp integrity and audit-trail completeness on Lazoo edits (who / what / when; original value retained).
4. To describe usability of Lazoo v1 using the System Usability Scale (Brooke, 1996) and three structured questions.

### Exploratory objectives

- To describe the difference between calculated TST and the CSD-M item-8 construct (subjective estimated time asleep / tst_subjective). Item 8 is not the primary endpoint.
- To describe nap/doze reporting and lifestyle text (caffeine, alcohol, sleep medication as user-entered text) as covariates only.

### What this study will not do

- Fill a clinical claim. Clinical claims: TBD — pending Sara paste.
- Test CBT-I, treatment responsiveness, or care pathways.
- Create a national registry.
- Validate an AI output or present this study as a mark. Clinical claims: TBD — pending Sara paste.
- Enrol NHS trust sites unless the register and this protocol are amended and HRA Approval is obtained.

## Study Population

*Purpose:* Define who may take part without implying a clinical population that the app is meant to diagnose.

Adults aged 18 years or over who can use the restricted English-language app and who are willing to complete both Lazoo and CSD-M each morning for 14 days. Participants are invited volunteers. They are not enrolled as NHS patients under this version, and no care will change because of diary values.

Target for feasibility: 30 completers. Screening may continue until that floor is met or the CI stops the study. The SAP may raise the n; it may not lower the completer definition without a protocol amendment.

A completer is a participant with at least 10 of 14 paired mornings that are evaluable for calculated TST on both diaries (see Endpoints).

Parent/carer completion and anyone under 18 are out of scope until a paediatric PIS exists.


## Inclusion Criteria

1. Aged ≥18 years at consent.

## Exclusion Criteria

*Purpose:* Exclude people for whom two diaries would be inappropriate or would duplicate another trial, not on diagnostic grounds for the app.

1. Unable to give informed consent.
2. Current participation in another sleep-diary research study that would duplicate the same nights.
3. CI or delegated researcher judges that completing two morning diaries for 14 days would be inappropriate for that person (for example acute distress; the reason is recorded as a file note, not as a diagnosis generated by Lazoo).
4. Unable to complete entries in English.
5. No access to a compatible device, or unwilling to use the unlisted / closed-testing app.
6. NHS-site enrolment (not permitted under this version).

People who currently have a sleep difficulty, or who attend a sleep clinic, are not excluded by that fact alone, and they are not enrolled in order to test a diagnostic claim. Self-reported background is descriptive only.


## Study Procedures

*Purpose:* Describe a 14-morning paired-diary path that cannot auto-fill the comparator and does not deliver scores or flags.

1. Invitation and screening against inclusion/exclusion. Participant ID format: LAZ-VAL-001-NNN (never reused).
2. Informed consent (written or REC-approved remote). Store the consent-pack version on the user record. Research contact on all participant-facing sheets: admin@sleepandhealth.org (not a personal Gmail). Do not use Chelsea PALS, UCLH PALS, or any NHS PALS line on ~~App Ltd~~ SHC Sleep Solutions Limited documents.
3. Issue study code / login. The app must not be publicly listed. Confirm app version against the LAZ-VER-001 pin.
4. Training (visit 0 / day 0): one walkthrough of CSD-M construct fields and of the Lazoo morning diary. CSD-M is completed on paper or a locked PDF so Lazoo cannot auto-fill it. in_bed and sleep_attempt are trained as two different clocks.
5. Mornings 1–14: CSD-M first, then Lazoo, same sitting. No in-app interpretation, score, flag, CBT-I lesson, or phenotyping output. Participants may edit Lazoo; edits are append-only with original retained.
6. Day 15 (or first working day after morning 14): return CSD-M source worksheets; complete SUS; complete three structured usability questions; optional short interview (audio only if separately consented; default is notes on the CRF). Confirm withdrawal wishes for toggle 3.
7. Close-out: export Lazoo JSON+CSV; file export receipt on the CRF; stop study access or leave the person on the restricted app for personal use under toggle 1 only, as they choose.

Toggle 2 (share with a nominated clinician) is optional and is not required for VAL-001. Any share is the history only, with no automated interpretation line.

Deviations (wrong order, Lazoo completed first, missed morning, lost PDF) are logged. A morning with Lazoo-first is not primary-evaluable unless the SAP defines a sensitivity rule.

## Endpoints

*Purpose:* For this agreement study, calculated TST is the draft primary study quantity. It is not the product claim. The product measure stays TBD — pending Sara paste. Keep the two bedtime clocks separate, and keep item 8 exploratory.

### Derived quantities (both diaries, same formula)

Clocks are datetimes on the diary_date (with previous-calendar-day allowed for in_bed / sleep_attempt). Durations are minutes.

- TWAK = out_of_bed − wake
- TST = (out_of_bed − sleep_attempt) − SOL − WASO − TWAK
- TIB = out_of_bed − in_bed
- SE = TST / TIB × 100
- sleep_onset (derived, not asked as a clock) = sleep_attempt + SOL

These TST, SOL, WASO, and SE formulas are draft study arithmetic for this agreement study. They are not the product claim. The product measure stays TBD — pending Sara paste.

in_bed and sleep_attempt must not be collapsed. Naps / dozes do not enter nocturnal TST. WASO is the total of awakenings excluding the final wake. Primary TWAK uses out_of_bed − wake, not CSD-M item 6b. Item 6b (twak_try_min) may be used only in a pre-specified sensitivity analysis if collected.

A primary-evaluable paired morning has, on both diaries: sleep_attempt, SOL, WASO, wake, out_of_bed, all internally consistent (out_of_bed ≥ wake ≥ sleep_attempt; SOL ≥ 0; WASO ≥ 0; TST ≥ 0). in_bed is required for TIB/SE secondary analyses and is still collected every morning.

### Primary endpoint

Night-level difference in calculated TST: Lazoo minus CSD-M, minutes. Analysis: Bland–Altman mean bias and 95% limits of agreement, with nights clustered within participant. No diagnostic cut-point. No claim that Lazoo is “valid for insomnia” or any other condition.

### Secondary endpoints

- Same Bland–Altman (and ICC, method in SAP) for SOL, WASO, SE, awakenings_n.
- Completeness: proportion of mornings with both diaries; item-level missingness; proportion of participants who are completers (≥10/14 evaluable paired mornings).
- Timestamp / audit: proportion of Lazoo entries with client and server timestamps; proportion of edits with who/what/when and original value retained.
- SUS total score (0–100); descriptive summaries of the three structured questions.

### Exploratory

Calculated TST versus tst_subjective (CSD-M item 8 construct) on each diary; between-diary difference on item 8. Lifestyle text and naps as covariates / descriptors only.

### Sample size

Feasibility floor: 30 completers. This is not the final n. The signed SAP will calculate n so that the 95% confidence interval around the TST limits of agreement is usable, with a sleep statistician. Until that SAP is signed, do not describe 30 as a powered Bland–Altman sample.

## Statistical Analysis Plan

*Purpose:* State the analysis skeleton and the sample-size floor, and leave precision calculations to a signed SAP.

A separate signed SAP will be filed before database lock and, for IRAS, before submission. Until then, the following is binding enough to design the CRF and DMP, but not a substitute for the SAP.

- Analysis populations: (i) all consented; (ii) completers (≥10/14 evaluable paired mornings) — feasibility population; (iii) all evaluable paired mornings.
- Primary: Bland–Altman of night-level calculated TST (Lazoo − CSD-M). Report mean bias, SD of differences, 95% limits of agreement. Clustering of nights within person will be handled by a method named in the SAP (for example Bland and Altman 2007 repeated-measures limits, or a mixed-effects residual SD).
