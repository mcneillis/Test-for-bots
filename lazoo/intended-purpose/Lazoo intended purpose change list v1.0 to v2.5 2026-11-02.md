# Change list: Lazoo intended purpose, version 1.0 to version 2.5

Version 2.5, dated 2 November 2026, supersedes version 1.0 of 12 October 2026.

**Manufacturer.** The manufacturer is SHC Sleep Solutions Limited (company number 12467350). The company name changed on 27 October 2026.

**Classification.** Class IIa under the Medical Devices Regulations 2002 (SI 2002/618), as amended.

**Purpose.** Insomnia monitoring only is replaced by measurement of circadian rhythm and sleep pattern analysis. The clinician receives the raw data and a report. Lazoo does not diagnose.

**Conditions.** The conditions monitored are all circadian rhythm disorders, insomnia, and co-morbid insomnia with sleep apnoea.

**Monitoring.** Lazoo monitors the initial sleep pattern and the efficacy of medications and therapies prescribed by clinicians.

**Description.** Lazoo is software as a medical device (SaMD). The CBT-14 is a separate medical device that monitors core body temperature via an AI algorithm, adds circadian rhythm tracking and uses an AI algorithm to estimate sleep stages from heart rate variability. It sends its data to Lazoo, adding to the raw data the sleep diary collects from the patient.

**Outputs.** The clinician receives the raw data and a report. The report uses artificial intelligence (AI) to calculate sleep efficiency, wake after sleep onset, time in bed, calories consumed, calories expended and the timing of medication. With the CBT-14, the report also includes circadian rhythm tracking and sleep stages estimated from heart rate variability by an AI algorithm.

**Contraindications.** Lazoo is not intended for people without a smartphone. Lazoo is not intended for people with a reaction to the adhesive used in the CBT-14.

**Pregnancy.** Lazoo is suitable for use in pregnancy. An independent validation study in pregnancy follows validation in adults aged 18 years and over. Pregnancy is excluded from the adult validation study.

**Unchanged.** Adults aged 18 years and over. Home use. Lazoo is not an emergency service. The clinician is the user of the outputs. No diagnosis is given to the patient.

**Not carried forward.** The description of the device as diary capture alone, the decision-support functions set out in version 1.0, the MHRA AI Airlock form text, the source list, the limits that placed circadian rhythm disorders and sleep apnoea outside the purpose, the “suspected or diagnosed insomnia” wording, the GP and specialist settings, and the “not yet UKCA-marked” line. Version 2.5 includes all circadian rhythm disorders and co-morbid insomnia with sleep apnoea.
