## Device description

Lazoo is software as a medical device (SaMD). The CBT-14 is a separate medical device that monitors core body temperature via an AI algorithm and sends its data to Lazoo, adding to the raw data the sleep diary collects from the patient. The CBT-14 adds circadian rhythm tracking and uses an AI algorithm to estimate sleep stages from heart rate variability.

## Intended purpose

Lazoo is intended for measurement of circadian rhythm and sleep pattern analysis. It monitors the initial sleep pattern and the efficacy of medications and therapies prescribed by clinicians. The clinician receives the raw data and a report. Lazoo does not diagnose.

## Intended users

The clinician is the user of the outputs.

## Patient population

Adults aged 18 years and over. Lazoo is suitable for use in pregnancy.

## Conditions monitored

Lazoo monitors all circadian rhythm disorders, insomnia, and co-morbid insomnia with sleep apnoea.

## Use environment

Lazoo is used at home.

## Outputs

The clinician receives the raw data and a report. The report uses artificial intelligence (AI) to calculate sleep efficiency, wake after sleep onset, time in bed, calories consumed, calories expended and the timing of medication. With the CBT-14, the report also includes circadian rhythm tracking and sleep stages estimated from heart rate variability by an AI algorithm.

## Contraindications and limitations

Lazoo is not intended for persons under 18 years of age. It does not give a diagnosis to the patient. It is not an emergency service. Pregnancy is not a contraindication. An independent validation study in pregnancy follows validation in adults aged 18 years and over. Pregnancy is excluded from the adult validation study.
