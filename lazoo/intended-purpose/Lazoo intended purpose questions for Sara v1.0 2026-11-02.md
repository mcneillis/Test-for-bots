# Lazoo intended purpose: questions for Sara

Version 1.0, 2 November 2026. Questions on the intended purpose, version 2.0. This note is not part of the statement.

1. The report uses artificial intelligence to calculate sleep metrics, sleep stages are estimated from heart rate variability by an artificial intelligence algorithm, and the CBT-14 is linked to the circadian measurement. Does any of this change the Class IIa rationale under UK MDR Rule 11 (software)?

2. How does the heart-rate amendment (AMD-01, attached as the LAZ-VAL-001 protocol amendment, version 0.6) relate to this purpose, given that heart rate is outside the validation study? Version 2.0 states heart rate variability and sleep-stage estimation, and it does not state heart rate.

3. Are calories consumed, calories expended and the timing of medication in scope as measured values, or as values entered by the user?

4. The clinician receives the raw data and formulates the report, and the report uses artificial intelligence to calculate the metrics. Is the device output the clinician’s report, a report generated by Lazoo, or both?

5. Co-morbid insomnia with sleep apnoea is included. Is sleep apnoea without insomnia outside the purpose?

6. What should the statement call the CBT-14, and is the sleep diary still part of the device?

7. Should the word “accurate” remain for CBT-14 tracking of the circadian rhythm?

8. The population is adults aged 18 years and over, and the conditions include all circadian rhythm disorders. Are disorders that occur under the age of 18 outside the purpose on grounds of age alone?

9. Pregnancy is within the purpose and outside the validation study. Do any pregnancy limits in AMD-01 apply to heart rate variability or to the sleep-stage estimate, and should they be written into the purpose?

10. Version 1.0 stated one product layer. The manufacturer was given as Sleep and Health Clinic App Ltd, and the Airlock form used “Limited”. Should version 2.0 state a product layer, which legal name should it use, and should the registered office be included?
