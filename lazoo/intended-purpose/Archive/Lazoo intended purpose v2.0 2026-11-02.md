## Device description

Lazoo is software as a medical device. The CBT-14 assists in the accurate tracking of the circadian rhythm.

## Intended purpose

Lazoo is intended for measurement of circadian rhythm and sleep pattern analysis. It monitors the initial sleep pattern and the efficacy of medications and therapies prescribed by the clinician. It assists the clinician via the report. Lazoo does not itself diagnose.

## Intended users

The clinician is the user of the outputs.

## Patient population

Adults aged 18 years and over, including in pregnancy.

## Conditions monitored

Lazoo monitors all circadian rhythm disorders, insomnia, and co-morbid insomnia with sleep apnoea.

## Use environment

Lazoo is used at home.

## Outputs

The clinician receives the raw data and formulates the report. The report uses artificial intelligence (AI) to calculate sleep efficiency, wake after sleep onset, time in bed, calories consumed, calories expended and the timing of medication. Sleep stages are estimated from heart rate variability by an AI algorithm.

## Contraindications and limitations

Lazoo is not intended for persons under 18 years of age. It does not give a diagnosis to the patient. It is not an emergency service. Pregnancy is not a contraindication. Pregnancy is excluded from the validation study because use in pregnancy is evaluated in a separate study.
