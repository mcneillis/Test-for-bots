# Change list: Lazoo intended purpose, version 1.0 to version 2.0

Version 2.0, dated 2 November 2026, supersedes version 1.0 of 12 October 2026.

**Purpose.** Insomnia monitoring only is replaced by measurement of circadian rhythm and sleep pattern analysis.

**Conditions.** The conditions monitored are all circadian rhythm disorders, insomnia, and co-morbid insomnia with sleep apnoea.

**Monitoring.** Lazoo monitors the initial sleep pattern and the efficacy of medications and therapies prescribed by the clinician.

**Outputs.** The clinician receives the raw data and formulates the report. The report uses artificial intelligence (AI) to calculate sleep efficiency, wake after sleep onset, time in bed, calories consumed, calories expended and the timing of medication. Sleep stages are estimated from heart rate variability by an AI algorithm. The CBT-14 assists in the accurate tracking of the circadian rhythm. Lazoo assists the clinician via the report and does not itself diagnose.

**Unchanged.** Adults aged 18 years and over. Pregnancy remains within the intended population and remains excluded from the validation study. Home use. Lazoo is not an emergency service. The clinician is the user of the outputs. No diagnosis is given to the patient. The classification is Class IIa.

**Not carried forward.** The description of the device as diary capture alone, the decision-support functions set out in version 1.0, the MHRA AI Airlock form text, the source list, and the limits that placed circadian rhythm disorders and sleep apnoea outside the purpose. Version 2.0 includes all circadian rhythm disorders and co-morbid insomnia with sleep apnoea.
