## General instructions for completing this booklet

This booklet records the study data for one participant. Complete it in line with the protocol and the data management plan, and use the guidance below for every page.

The booklet records the constructs of the Consensus Sleep Diary–Morning (CSD-M) as clock times, durations, counts and ratings, and leaves out the CSD-M question wording. The exact question wording and the Carney PDF will be filed only once an industry licence from Dr Colleen Carney is on file (Carney et al., Sleep 2012;35:287–302, doi:10.5665/sleep.1642).

The booklet records study data only and supports no clinical claim; the sponsor will confirm the wording of any clinical claims. Record only the fields listed here. The booklet has no place for scores, flags, content on cognitive behavioural therapy for insomnia or sleep phenotyping. The fields vitals.core_temp, vitals.wearable and ai_event are reserved and stay empty.

The participant ID format is `LAZ-VAL-001-NNN`. A completer is a participant with at least 10 of 14 paired mornings that can be evaluated for calculated total sleep time.

Write clearly in black ink. Record clock times in 24-hour HH:MM format and durations in minutes. Correct an error by drawing a single line through it, writing the correct value beside it, and adding your initials and the date; the original entry must remain legible. Where a value is missing, tick "Missing" rather than leaving the box blank. ### Calculated fields (computed in the same way for both diaries, and never asked of the participant)

- TWAK = out_of_bed − wake
- TST = (out_of_bed − sleep_attempt) − SOL − WASO − TWAK   ← PRIMARY
- TIB = out_of_bed − in_bed
- SE = TST / TIB × 100
- sleep_onset (derived) = sleep_attempt + SOL

TST (total sleep time) is the primary quantity. These formulas for total sleep time, sleep onset latency, wake after sleep onset and sleep efficiency are draft study arithmetic for this agreement study and are not a product claim. The sponsor will set the product measure separately.
