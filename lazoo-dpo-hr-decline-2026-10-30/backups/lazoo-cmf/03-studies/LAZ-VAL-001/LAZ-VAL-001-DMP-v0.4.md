## Collection

*Purpose:* Describe how paired mornings are captured so Lazoo cannot auto-fill CSD-M, and how TST is derived.

### Order of capture

Each morning the participant completes CSD-M first, then Lazoo, in the same sitting. Training states that in_bed and sleep_attempt are two clocks. Lazoo-first is a deviation and is not primary-evaluable by default.

### Lazoo

Participants make entries on their own device, using the study code. Fields follow the data dictionary and the protocol construct map. Edits are allowed and are kept in an append-only audit trail recording who, what and when, with the original value retained (ALCOA+). The data are exported as JSON and CSV at close-out and whenever the Chief Investigator requests. CSD-M field 15 (Comments, `notes`) is not collected and is excluded from the CRF and the Lazoo export. CSD-M licence terms are still to be confirmed. If the licence requires field 15, it would be kept with an on-form instruction (“Do not enter names, contact details or other identifying information; describe sleep only”), a 200-character limit, a redaction review at upload and the free-text strip, and it would be covered in the DPIA. Charts are not a substitute for raw timestamps. The fields vitals.core_temp[], vitals.wearable[] and ai_event[] are reserved empty in v1.

### CSD-M

The source is a locked PDF or electronic form, uploaded electronically by the participant and carrying the study ID only (no names, dates of birth or free-text identifiers); the licensed instrument is used once the Carney industry licence is filed. No paper copies are posted and no photographs are taken. Values are transcribed onto the case report form daily construct page by double entry with a discrepancy log, and discrepancies are logged and resolved before database lock.

### Derived quantities (compute on export / analysis file; store raw clocks too)

- TWAK = out_of_bed − wake
- TST = (out_of_bed − sleep_attempt) − SOL − WASO − TWAK  (PRIMARY; not CSD-M item 8)
- TIB = out_of_bed − in_bed
- SE = TST / TIB × 100
- sleep_onset = sleep_attempt + SOL

These total sleep time, sleep onset latency, wake after sleep onset, and SE formulas are draft study arithmetic for this agreement study. They are not the product claim. The product measure stays to be confirmed by the sponsor.

A primary-evaluable paired morning has sleep_attempt, sleep onset latency, wake after sleep onset, wake, out_of_bed on both diaries, internally consistent, total sleep time ≥ 0. Naps do not enter nocturnal total sleep time. Wake after sleep onset excludes the final wake.

### Usability

System Usability Scale (Brooke 1996) and three structured questions on the case report form at day 15. Entered once; no in-app score shown to the participant as a clinical output.

### Consent collection

Toggle wording version stored. Withdrawal of toggle 3 stops future research use; participant information sheet states that a locked analysis set is not unpicked. Toggle 4 not collected on by default.

To be confirmed: the electronic case report form supplier and its validation. Validation will follow the Medicines and Healthcare products Regulatory Agency ‘GXP’ Data Integrity Guidance and Definitions (March 2018) and ICH E6(R3) on computerised systems, with 21 CFR Part 11 only where a US submission is planned. Paper case report form + Lazoo export is the default collection model.

## Quality Control

*Purpose:* Specify checks that support measurement agreement, not diagnostic accuracy.

- Consent: 100% of completers have toggles 1 and 3, wording version, and date.
- Pairing log: every morning coded evaluable or not, with deviation code if needed.
- CSD-M transcription: double entry before lock; discrepancy log closed.
- Lazoo: export contains raw timestamps; in_bed distinct from sleep_attempt; audit trail on edits; v1 reserved fields empty.
- Derived TST recomputed independently from raw clocks in the analysis file; do not trust a pre-computed app TST if one is ever present (v1 should not display a clinical score).
- Range checks: SOL, WASO, TWAK ≥ 0; out_of_bed ≥ wake ≥ sleep_attempt; TIB > 0 for SE; flag TST < 0 or > TIB for query — queries are data queries, not clinical flags to the participant.
- SUS: 10 items present or missingness explained; score calculated centrally.
- App version on each export matches the LAZ-VER-001 pin. The pinned version is to be confirmed.

Database lock: Chief Investigator + data manager (when named) sign that pairing, transcription, and export receipts are complete. Unlock only with a file-noted reason.

Quality control is never aimed at “correcting” diaries toward a diagnosis. Queries go to source (participant worksheet or Lazoo audit), not to a clinician interpretation module.
