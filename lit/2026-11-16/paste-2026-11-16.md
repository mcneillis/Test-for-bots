# Sleep papers, 9–16 November 2026

Plain-English note. Abstract level. Not medical advice.

## Six points

1. Two trials of the oral drug orforglipron cut breathing pauses by about 12 to 13 an hour more than placebo, in adults with overweight or obesity and moderate to severe sleep apnoea.
2. Twelve weeks of jaw and tongue exercises did not beat a waiting list for pauses, oxygen, or sleepiness in mild to moderate sleep apnoea.
3. Digital insomnia programmes improved scores on average across 44 trials, but the spread was too wide to treat them as a standard stand-in for talking therapy.
4. Short trials of orexin-2 drugs improved wakefulness and cut cataplexy in narcolepsy type 1. One drug in that class was stopped for liver injury.
5. In UK Biobank, about 7 hours in bed sat at the lower-risk part of the curve for later death, and a single week of wrist data was fairly stable for sleep length but not for social jetlag.
6. No paper on sleep-related painful erections. In a São Paulo adult sample, reflux symptoms were not linked with sleep bruxism once other factors were included.
